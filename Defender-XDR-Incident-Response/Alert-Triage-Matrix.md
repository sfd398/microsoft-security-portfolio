# Alert Triage Matrix

| Alert | Severity | Response Time |
|---------|---------|---------|
| User Reported Phish | Medium | 4 Hours |
| Malware Detected | High | 1 Hour |
| Credential Theft | High | 1 Hour |
| Impossible Travel | Medium | 4 Hours |
| MFA Fatigue Attack | High | 1 Hour |
| Privileged Account Compromise | Critical | Immediate |

## Escalation Criteria

### Critical

- Domain administrator account
- Multiple compromised users
- Ransomware indicators

### High

- Credential compromise
- Malware execution
- Suspicious mailbox activity
