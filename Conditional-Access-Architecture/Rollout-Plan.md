# Conditional Access Rollout Plan

## Phase 1

### Report Only

Evaluate impact of policies before enforcement.

Duration: 2 Weeks

---

## Phase 2

### Pilot Group

10 Volunteer Members

Objectives:

- Verify MFA
- Verify sign-in experience
- Validate exclusions

Duration: 2 Weeks

---

## Phase 3

### Officer Group

Department Officers

Objective:

Protect privileged access.

Duration: 1 Week

---

## Phase 4

### Department Wide Deployment

All Members

Duration: 1 Week

---

## Success Criteria

- Zero Legacy Authentication
- MFA Adoption > 95%
- No Critical Access Disruptions
