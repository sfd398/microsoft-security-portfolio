# Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Password compromise | High | High | MFA |
| Phishing attacks | High | High | Defender for Office 365 |
| Unmanaged devices | High | Medium | Intune Compliance Policies |
| Excessive permissions | Medium | Medium | PIM and Role Reviews |
| Account takeover | High | Medium | Conditional Access |
| Data loss | High | Medium | Backup and Retention Policies |

1. Password attacks
